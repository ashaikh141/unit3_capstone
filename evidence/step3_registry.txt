- sql_agent: Answers questions about STRUCTURED records in the company database: clients (name, industry, region, contract value), projects (status, dates, budget), consultants (grade, specialisation, billable hours, project), and invoices (amount, status, due date, paid date). Use it for counts, totals, sums, averages, statuses, dates, amounts, rankings, and who is assigned to what. NOT for policies, processes, guidelines, or anything written in company documents.
- retrieval_agent: Answers questions from the firm's WRITTEN policy documents: onboarding, invoicing and payment policy, project delivery process, and other guidelines. NOT for counting or totaling records, looking up specific clients, projects, invoices, or consultants, or any number stored in the database.
